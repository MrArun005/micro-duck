What you're seeing

The body is a 2 kg inverted pendulum on two legs. The ankles can only shift the pressure inside the soles, so to stop after a push the duck has to put a foot down near the capture point. Too hard a shove and it can't reach: it tumbles as a rigid body with ground contact and friction, then rolls itself back up with limited motor torque.

Walking is a model-based controller, not a trained policy (yet).
